Managerial Agent System
Multi-agent orchestration for small business operations
Prototype  ·  Deploying Soon
A hierarchical multi-agent system built around a central Managerial Agent that orchestrates four specialized sub-agents running in parallel. Each sub-agent monitors a specific operational domain — phone queue, financials, HR compliance, and marketing — and reports structured summaries upward. The Managerial Agent synthesizes these inputs into a daily executive digest with prioritized escalations, giving small business owners a clear picture of what needs attention without manually tracking every system. Designed and prototyped for a law office use case, with plans to generalize across small business verticals.
